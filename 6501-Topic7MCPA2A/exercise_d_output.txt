# ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models

## Summary
The paper **"ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models"** (2022) introduces a novel approach that interleaves reasoning and action in large language models (LLMs). This methodology, known as ReAct, improves the ability of LLMs to perform complex decision-making tasks by generating reasoning traces that enhance task-specific actions. The authors demonstrate that this integration leads to better interpretability and effectiveness in various tasks, such as question answering and interactive decision making, surpassing state-of-the-art benchmarks and addressing common issues like hallucination and error propagation.
