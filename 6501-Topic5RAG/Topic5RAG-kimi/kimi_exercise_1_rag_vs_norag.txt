--- WITH RAG (top_k=5) ---
Response time: 5.01s
Answer:
The Ford Model T Service Manual provides detailed instructions for fixing a slipping transmission band:

SYMPTOMS: The car fails to move when the pedal is pressed, or the pedal goes to the floor without engaging.

CAUSE: The transmission band is worn or has become loose.

PROCEDURE:
1. Remove the transmission cover by unscrewing the bolts around the perimeter.

2. Locate the three transmission bands: the low speed band, the reverse band, and the brake band.

3. To tighten a band, turn the adjusting screw clockwise. Each band has an external adjusting screw accessible from the driver's compartment.

4. Tighten the screw one notch at a time until the slipping stops.

5. CAUTION: Do not overtighten as this will cause premature wear and may make the pedal hard to operate.

6. Test the car after each adjustment.

If the band is worn out, it must be replaced with a new band. The manual recommends inspecting bands every 5,000 miles.
